  says "a seeded replay" out loud. Never implied to be organic.
- The secret appears in **context B's** request line. It was *issued* to A; A's log line is
  the page request, so the marked string is in B only. Narrate it that way.

- Two contexts are not two actors. That is the point of the corpus result, not a caveat
  bolted on at the end.
